## Detailed Classification Criteria

### 1. 🔴 The Legislative Dependency Trap (Automatic KILL)
* **Definition:** A business model that cannot legally operate or bill customers until a government ministry, legislative body, or municipal council amends an existing law or retires a mandatory statutory process.
* **Warning Signs:**
  * *"We will lobby the government to accept our sensor instead of the mandatory physical sample."*
  * *"We will create a digital exchange for rights that currently require discretionary ministerial approval."*
  * *"We will get a special exemption from the environmental act."*
* **The Strategic Reality:** Civil servants and politicians face fierce pressure from environmental, labor, or corporate lobbies. Navigating a legislative overhaul takes years of capital-intensive lobbying with near-zero probability of early startup success.

### 2. 🟡 Technical Certification (Feasible Friction & Defensive Moat)
* **Definition:** The regulatory framework **already explicitly provides a pathway** for commercial equipment, software, or methods to be approved by an accredited testing authority (e.g., ISO-17025 accreditation, DLG type-approval, CE medical device marking, TÜV certification).
* **Warning Signs / Characteristics:**
  * *"We need 9 months of testing with Wageningen UR or TÜV to achieve formal type-approval."*
  * *"The testing regime costs €50,000–€100,000 in testing lab fees."*
* **The Strategic Reality:** **DO NOT AUTO-KILL.** While technical certification creates timeline and capital friction, clearing it establishes a formidable **moat** against copycats. Incorporate the testing timeline and costs directly into the venture runway model.

### 3. 🟢 Regulatory Arbitrage (Automatic REINFORCE)
* **Definition:** The government has **already enacted strict laws**, authorities are **actively levying fines or withholding licenses**, and the startup’s product operates 100% within the current statutory formulas to help the customer legally minimize penalties or maximize statutory bonuses.
* **Characteristics:**
  * Uses the government’s *own official calculation protocols* (e.g., Dutch KringloopWijzer, corporate CSRD Scope 3 reporting, IRS section tax credits).
  * Direct, immediate cash impact: Avoids a €30/m³ fine or unlocks a 1.5 ct/kg quality bonus.
  * Zero permits, zero legislative lobbying, zero new technical accreditation required.
